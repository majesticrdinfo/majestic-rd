About Majestic R&D

A physician supply company
built on technology that didn't exist yet.

Majestic R&D is a biotech and research company based in Maryland. We develop and manufacture proprietary delivery technology and supply research-grade compounds to licensed physicians and other customers — built on science developed outside the standard pharmaceutical pipeline and validated by independent third-party laboratories.

Our current portfolio spans three areas: AIM Therapeutix, a proprietary intranasal delivery platform we manufacture in-house at our laboratory on the UMBC campus; a peptide program supplying semaglutide, tirzepatide, and retatrutide to licensed physician practices through our partner Pro Peptide Solutions in South Carolina; and a topical CBG formulation in development.

Every peptide lot is tested by TrustPointe Analytics and Chromate — independent third-party laboratories. COAs include a QR code linking directly to the lab's own verification portal. We do not ask physicians to take our word for purity or identity.

What We Are — And What We Are Not

We are a biotech company.
The current products are the proof of concept.

Majestic R&D is not a compounding pharmacy, a clinical research organization, or a standard distribution company. We are a biotech and research company that develops proprietary technology, manufactures it in-house, and brings it to market through multiple channels depending on the product.

AIM is available to licensed physicians and business partners — no patient-direct supply at this time. Peptides are physician-access only under RUO designation. CBG topical is in development. Each product operates under its own access and compliance model.

The technology we've built — specifically AIM — is not a sourced product. It is a proprietary intranasal delivery platform manufactured in our own laboratory on the UMBC campus, with a mechanism that targets CNS delivery through a combination of principles most pharmaceutical engineers have not combined.

The physician supply program and direct channels are the current go-to-market. The underlying asset is the intellectual property and manufacturing capability behind them.

His work spans neurology, advanced therapeutic delivery systems, neurodegenerative recovery, nanoscience, energy systems, and applied defense research — with a specific focus on solving problems where conventional approaches have already been exhausted.
