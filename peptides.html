Program Eligibility

Who this program is designed for.

Eligible Practices
Licensed physician practices
B2B supply enrollment is open to licensed physicians, DOs, NPs, and PAs with an active professional license. License verification required on file prior to first order.
Supply Scope
RUO procurement only
All compounds are supplied under Research Use Only designation directly to licensed practices. B2B only — no patient-direct supply, no retail. Physician determines all clinical application under their professional license.
Not eligible: Practices without a licensed physician, DO, NP, or PA with an active professional license on file. Retail or direct-to-consumer procurement is not available under any circumstance.
