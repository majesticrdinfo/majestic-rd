Is this actually legal for my practice?
Yes. AIM is a non-sterile topical classified under USP-795 — not a drug, not a prescription product. Peptides are supplied B2B under RUO designation. You determine use under your own professional license, exactly as you do with bacteriostatic water or any compounding vehicle.

No. AIM requires zero facility investment — it’s non-sterile classification. Peptides ship from an ISO Class 5 facility in South Carolina. You receive inventory, compliance documentation, and training. Standard Luer-lock nasal atomizer is the only required equipment for AIM.

AIM Therapeutix

Direct CNS delivery. No needle. No sterile facility.

AIM is a non-Newtonian intranasal medium that delivers APIs via the olfactory and trigeminal nerve pathways — bypassing the blood-brain barrier entirely. The physician determines what to use it with, under their own professional license.

Non-sterile classification. No USP-797 required. Supplied directly to licensed physicians as a standalone delivery vehicle — the same model as bacteriostatic water.

Published research documents improved CNS bioavailability versus the subcutaneous route. Real-world observational reports across 10 de-identified cases spanning TBI, PTSD, post-stroke recovery, and inflammatory conditions describe consistent tolerance and rapid onset. Full reference documents available to qualified physician inquiries.

Compliance position: AIM is a non-sterile compounding medium for topical intranasal use. Not a drug. Not a prescription product. All therapeutic use governed by physician license.
